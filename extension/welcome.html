Other stuff you might want to know, and the legal stuff

What alerts allow. Good Codes checks the address of each site you visit against its list of shops, on your computer. Only listed shops' names are ever sent, when you're at their checkout. It shows a small banner on those shops' cart and checkout pages, and never reads the page. Chrome's permission prompt sounds broader than this; the code is public if you want to check.

Without alerts it can't see the sites you visit at all. The icon still turns green on shops with codes; click it to see them.
